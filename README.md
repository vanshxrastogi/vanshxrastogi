I’m an independent AI filmmaker based in **Prayagraj, India**, creating cinematic brand stories, product visuals and original short-form films. I bring together concept development, creative direction, reference-driven generation and thoughtful editing, sound and color.

- **Brand films & AI commercials** — clear hooks, product storytelling and cinematic visuals.
- **CGI & product films** — controlled lighting, materials, composition and motion.
- **Micro dramas & creative reels** — expressive characters, connected scenes and visual worlds.
- **Pre-wedding films** — emotional pacing and an intentional cinematic mood.
- **Creative direction & finishing** — concepts, scripts, storyboards, editing and sound.
